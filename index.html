Smart Parking Finder

CarCompass

This project aims to solve the problem of people when they struggle to locate their parked vehicles in large or shifting parking areas.

The primary objective is to use OCR-based number plate detection and slot indication to pinpoint a vehicle's position and guide the user to it. This solution is relevant to modern smart-city systems, offering improved convenience and management.
